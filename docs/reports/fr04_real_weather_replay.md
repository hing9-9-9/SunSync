## Limitations

- **One Scene, one point.** The result belongs to the example Scene of `docs/examples` (one window, protected regions A to D, caps of the app policy) at the KMA point in Seoul. It does not describe every room.
- **The observation is treated as a perfect forecast.** NFR1.2 speaks about forecasts, which are less steady than observations; read these numbers as the most the stabilization can do on that day.
- **DSR is not DNI.** The observed downward shortwave radiation is converted with the Erbs decomposition (`dsr_to_dni_physics`); the conversion error is part of every number.
- **The 5-minute step is interpolated.** KMA gives one value per 10 minutes; the Scene interpolates linearly between them, so the 5-minute case is not an independent measurement. It also turns a dip of one 10-minute observation into a dip of one 5-minute step. The 10-minute rows use the observations as they are: a difference between the 5-minute and the 10-minute rows is an effect of the interpolation, not of the weather.
- **Label.** The Scene only accepts the weather sources `synthetic`, `open_meteo` and `pvlib_clear_sky`; the replayed values carry `synthetic` although they are observations.
- **Hours.** Only 08:00 to 16:50 Korean time of each day is replayed (KMA has DSR from 07:40 to 16:50), not the whole day.
- **No forecast band.** The replay passes no uncertainty band, so the hysteresis margin of FR-04.3 uses the median value only.
