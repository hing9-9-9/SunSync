## Limitations

- The irradiance is synthetic (sine-based), not measured. It shows how the controllers behave on a made-up sky, not how much they would help on a real day.
- The 'FR-02 rule baseline' here is a three-line rule (opaque above the cap), not the real FR-02 planner. The API in `backend/app/api/adaptive_control.py` does compare against the real FR-02 plan.
- The forecast band (q10, q50, q90) is made up. The real band width comes from the FR-01.4 model and may be narrower or wider.
- The cap (50 W/m2) and the optical profile are assumptions. The cap should follow the grade policy of the scene and the film values should come from a measured film. No SGF datasheet with transmittance, haze or response time was found (see `docs/fr04_adaptive_control_lg_sgf.md`).
- The model treats a film as lowering direct irradiance. A scattering film (PDLC) turns direct light into diffuse light and may block much less total energy than this assumes.
- Steps are 5 minutes; the nowcast refreshes every 10 minutes, and the minimum hold and gap rules are 10 minutes.
- Opacity is used as a proxy for daylight.
